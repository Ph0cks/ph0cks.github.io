about me

I'm Jason Moore, a Graphic Designer from Louisville, Kentucky with a background in print design and a little over a year of experience working in digital marketing with Cape & Bay. My day to day consists of creating ads, animations and other marketing collateral for high production health and fitness brands, retail clients and charitable organizations.

Outside of the office I love to rock climb, though the precision, mindfulness and visual imagery of it have found their way into the observed experience of my work. I want to create an almost meditative space for the viewer to feel the same sense of focus and clarity, free of unnecessary distractions, as I do while climbing. Thoughtful image selection, dynamic abstract elements and strong informational heirarchy allow me to create clean compositions that communicate information in a calm and conscious manner.
