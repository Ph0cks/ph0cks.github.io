Bird-of-Paradise Exhibit Placard

This design was created for a zoo exhibit, to be posted on a placard outside of the enclosure. The project was free-reign so I chose an animal that has something interesting about it, in this case the King of Saxony Bird-of-Paradise. It has two antenna-like plumes that are able to be moved by the bird. I used the shape of the plumage to create modules for the information and the colors of the bird to create the color palette.
