No Shave Kit

This is a design for a fictional product released by Dollar Shave Club. The No Shave Kit is a promotional item released around November for, "No shave November," consisting of a beard brush and comb. The diecut is a no symbol that is repeated as a pattern on the inside of the package and in the "O" in NO. This design focuses on color, pattern and a diecut, all of which are done with the intention of adding interest.
