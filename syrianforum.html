Syrian Forum USA

Syrian Forum USA is a non-profit organization that provides direct aid, education, housing and much more to displaced refugees in Syria. The primary goal in designing for Syrian Forum was to bring the viewer into the life of Syrian refugees through powerful infographics, impactful photography and compelling stories. Syria Forum has seen a major impact in the amount of donations recieved through our efforts and thousands of Syrian lives have been changed in the process.
