Alex Honnold illustration

This project was to create an editorial illustration relating to lifestyles or politics. I chose to illustrate a person who has recently become one of my inspirations, Alex Honnold. He is a rock climber that has become world famous for climbing the 3000 foot face of El Capitan in Yosemite National Park without a rope or harness. This is known as free-solo climbing and as the illustration depicts it is a fairly lofty idea. Honnold definitely has his head in the clouds, literally and figuratively, and is unafraid to reach for the sky.
