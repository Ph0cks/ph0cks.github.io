sketchbooks

This is a collection of hand illustrated type and imagery based on the work of other designers such as; Neville Brody, David Carson and Gail Anderson to name a few. They are accompanied by a fact about the referenced artist and a question to ask my professor. Each is associated with two to three words from which the imagery is drawn and a design principle to be followed. I enjoy the process of the hand and feel that this is an area that I am comfortable and create some of my best work.
