Swerve

I was given the opportunity to create a small run of advertisements for a New Year campaign for Swerve, a cycling studio located in New York City. These advertisements use dynamic linear elements and clean composition to create a feeling of freshness and reference the idea of completing resolutions in the New Year. I also designed a couple of "ticker-tape" style animated ads as a nod to Wall Street, another high intensity location in town.
