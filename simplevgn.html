Simple Vgn

I had the opportunity to help a small vegan meal replacement startup, simple vgn, realize their packaging design. The vegan food industry has a fresh, organic and fairly new perception. A modern feel is conveyed through typography, shape, and bold photography. While form, composition and illustration dance across the packaging and engage the viewer sensually. It's a simply sophisticated package for a simply wonderful business.
